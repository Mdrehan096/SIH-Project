# RETRACK Project Terminology & Definitions

## Key Railway Terminology
- **TMS**: Track Management System (Civil track engineering jobs).
- **TDMS**: Track Defect Management System (Ultrasonic flaw alerts & cracks).
- **SMMS**: Signal & Mechanical Management System (Electrical OHE & S&T).
- **COA**: Control Office Application (Live train tracking & schedules).
- **CP-SAT**: Constraint Programming SAT solver from Google OR-Tools.
- **Traffic Block**: Period when train movement on a track is suspended for maintenance.
- **Power Block**: Period when traction power on OHE catenary wires is switched off.
- **Joint Possession**: Synchronized block where multiple departments work simultaneously on the same section.
- **Digital PN**: 2-Factor Private Number verification code for block granting/clearing.
- **Safety Buffer**: Mandatory 15-minute buffer zone around express train passages.
