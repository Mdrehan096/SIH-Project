# Spatial & Temporal Maintenance Bundling

## 5 km Spatial Corridor Bundling
- Maintenance activities located within a **5.0 kilometer spatial radius** are automatically grouped into single joint possession corridors.
- Multi-department teams (Civil tamping crew, Electrical OHE wire crew, Signal test crew) occupy the track section during the exact same time window.
- **Downtime Reduction**: Reduces total corridor possession downtime by up to **40%** compared to uncoordinated isolated blocks.

## Temporal Windowing
- Maintenance windows are aligned into low-traffic overnight periods (typically 01:00 AM – 04:00 AM).
- Synchronizes traffic blocks, power blocks, and signal interlocks simultaneously.
